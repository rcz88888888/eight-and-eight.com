01 / ABOUT

Eight&Eight

Eight&Eight is a marketing agency operating at the intersection of technology and creative development.

We develop structures for ideas with the potential to become brands, platforms, products and new forms of interaction.

Our approach combines strategic thinking with creative execution besides analysis. We consider positioning, audience, mechanics, technology and market dynamics as parts of one System.

Strategycreates direction.
Mathematicscreates structure.
Creativitycreates distinction.

Eight&Eight is built for projects that require more than conventional marketing.

We work with founders, companies, creative professionals and technology-driven ventures to transform complex ideas into clear strategic concepts and scalable structures.
